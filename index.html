Educational reference — not a clinical authority

HUMAN 100 is a learning aid, not a diagnostic system, treatment guide, prescribing reference or substitute for professional judgment. The supplied clinical source documents have not been independently verified for this website; content, thresholds, rules and calculations may be wrong, incomplete or outdated. Verify every item against current, authentic, authoritative sources, the local laboratory reference interval and the person’s full clinical context.

Do not use a score, calculation, search result or differential list on its own to diagnose, rule out an emergency, decide treatment or determine disposition. Drug-specific dosing, contraindications, interactions and renal/hepatic adjustments are not populated here. Seek urgent professional assessment for emergency symptoms or red flags.

Do not enter names, dates of birth, medical record numbers or other patient-identifiable information. The symptom notes are temporary on-screen inputs and are not saved. Favorites and recent items are stored only in this browser.
